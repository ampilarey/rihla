# Families

Batch C from the Hijaz comparison, as the owner chose: **a family is the
travellers one person booked onto one departure. It is shown as one group
on the booking, roomed and bussed together by default, and headed by one
of them, who holds the family's documents and is the one we call.** It
uses the customer and traveller records that already exist. There is no
new login and no new portal.

This is not the Family Portal, the page a pilgrim gives relatives at
home. That keeps its name and its own links.

## Who is in a family

Everybody the same customer booked onto the same departure, on any of
their bookings that is travelling: held, confirmed or home. A relative
added a week later on a second booking is in the same family. A cancelled
or expired booking is not, and neither is the same customer's booking on
another departure.

## The head

The head is the first person on the booking when it is made, at the desk
or on the website. Where a family spans two bookings, it is the head of
the earlier one.

The **Family** button on a booking changes it:

- **Head of the family** can be anybody in the family, including somebody
  on the customer's other booking. The family has one head across all its
  bookings.
- **To the person who booked**: husband or wife, son or daughter, father
  or mother, brother or sister, another relative, or not related. The
  page then reads *Hassan Ahmed's father*, *Hassan Ahmed's sister*.
- **Phone on the journey**: a number that works where they are going.
  Give the head one. The others need one only if they carry their own.

The desk's *New traveller* form asks for the relationship and the phone
too.

## Who we call

The booking shows **Who we call**: the head, on their own phone. When the
head has no phone on file, it shows the booker's number and says that the
head's is missing.

Everywhere a phone is printed for a traveller, it is their own if they
have one, or else their family head's, or else the booker's:

- the office list;
- the bus list;
- the documents list;
- the office copy of the rooming list;
- the Scan page.

The Scan page also names the family head and says whose number it is. A
lost child's tag rings the head, not a son at home in Malé.

The payment list still shows the booker, who pays.

## Together by default

- **Buses**: *Add people* brings the rest of each chosen person's family,
  from whichever bus they are on now. Switch off *Bring the rest of their
  family* to split a family on purpose. A family split across two buses
  is still reported on the board and on the bus screen, including a
  family on two bookings.
- **Rooms**: *Put somebody in* brings the rest of their family who have
  no room in that hotel yet. Switch it off to room somebody apart on
  purpose, a grown son with the men for example.
- **The rooming check** reports a child with no adult *of their own
  family* in the room. A stranger's company is not supervision. Before
  this, any adult in the room was enough.

## On the lists

The office list, bus list, documents list and the office copy of the
rooming list keep each family together, with its head first and marked
**(head)**. The airline, hotel and ministry lists are not marked: their
name column is the name as on the passport.
